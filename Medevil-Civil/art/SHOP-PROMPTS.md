# The Shops — ChatGPT prompts

Four shop cards. Everyone starts with the **Builder's Yard**. The other three are
handed out by the dealer the moment you finish the building that unlocks them.

**Gold is the only money.** At the start of your turn the dealer pays you
**3 gold plus 1 for every hex you hold**, and everything in every shop is priced
in it. No food, wood or stone to count — one pile of coins per player.

Combat is a **damage count** — no range, no line of sight, no arrow rules. Every
unit is worth a number, you add up both sides, and the dice settle it. A knight
is not "better at reaching things", it is simply worth more in the count.

The one exception worth keeping, because it is the best bit of the video game:
**balloons fly over walls, and only archers and towers can shoot them down.**

---

## Before you paste anything

ChatGPT **garbles small text in pictures** — it will misspell "battering" or turn
a 3 into an 8. So ask it for the **card art with an empty list area**, and let me
lay the words in crisply afterwards. Prompts below are written that way.

If you would rather try the all-in-one version, add this to the end of any prompt:
*"Render the item list as readable text exactly as written above, spelled
correctly."* Then check every number before you print 4 copies.

## How to run them

Each prompt below is complete — copy the whole block, nothing to assemble.

Do **number 1 first**. Once you like it, put *"Match the style of the previous
image exactly."* at the top of 2, 3 and 4. That single line is what stops them
looking like four different games.

## How the shops work at the table

- **The dealer** — whoever is sitting out as Rules Helper — keeps all the shop
  cards in a pile and hands one over the moment somebody finishes the building.
- Everyone gets their **own copy** of each shop they unlock, so nobody has to
  wait to read it.
- You can only buy from a shop you have unlocked, and only on your **build
  token**.
