QUESTIONS

How do I find my world's seed?
On Java Edition, open chat and type /seed. If cheats are off, open the pause menu → Open to LAN → turn cheats on → then /seed works. On Bedrock, it's shown in the world settings — but see the Bedrock question below.
I went to a marker and the structure isn't there!
SeedSeeker knows every spot where the game tries to place a structure — that math is exact. But the game also checks the biome: a village can't spawn in the middle of the ocean, and a witch hut needs a swamp. If a spot came up empty, the biome didn't fit — just head to the next-nearest marker. Most of the time the first or second one is real.
Why can't it show me exact diamond blocks?
Ore placement depends on how the caves carved out the stone — the game decides ore spots while it builds the terrain, so no seed tool (not even the famous ones) can list exact ore blocks without simulating the whole world. What always works: the Ore Cheat Sheet above. Dig at the best Y-level for what you want and you'll hit it fast. For diamonds, bring an iron pickaxe or better, and mine at the Y-level shown for your version.
Does this work for Bedrock / PE / console?
No — this tool speaks Java Edition math. Bedrock places structures with different rules, so the markers won't line up. (Fun fact: slime chunks on Bedrock don't even use your seed!)
How accurate is the stronghold marker?
Strongholds are placed in rings around the world center at a random angle — SeedSeeker computes that exactly, but the game then nudges each one to nearby valid terrain, so treat the marker as "within a couple hundred blocks." Get close, then throw Eyes of Ender and follow them down.
