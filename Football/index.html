HOW TO PLAY

Chess meets football. Take turns doing one action: move a player, pass, or kick. Score touchdowns (7) and field goals (3) — first to 21 wins.

PLAYERS & MOVEMENT

QB Quarterback — up to 2 squares any direction. Cannon arm: passes up to 6 squares.
RB Running Back — jumps like a chess knight, leaping right over the line.
WR Wide Receiver — up to 3 squares diagonally, or 1 square straight. Route runners.
LB Linebacker — up to 3 squares horizontally/vertically.
DL Lineman — up to 2 squares horizontally/vertically. The wall.
S Safety — up to 2 squares any direction. Last line of defense.

THE BALL

  • Tackle = fumble: land your move exactly on the ball carrier — you strip the ball and they get shoved aside. Anyone standing right next to the carrier can always tackle, no matter how they normally move.
  • Pass: the QB throws to any teammate within 6 squares, right over his own linemen — but a defender standing on the throwing lane bats it down. Everyone else laterals 3 squares in a straight/diagonal line with a clear path.
  • Touchdown: run the ball into the end zone — or send a receiver into the end zone and hit them with a pass. 7 points.
  • Field goal: within 8 squares of the end zone, click the goalpost. Green = good. If a defender is next to the kicker it's BLOCKED and they take the ball. 3 points.

TIPS

  • Park your linemen on the lanes in front of your QB — passes can't go through bodies.
  • The RB's knight-jump ignores blockers. Nobody is safe.
  • Sneak a WR into the end zone a turn early, then throw the fade.
