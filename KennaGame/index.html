The Last Knight

In a single night, the sorcerer Concur crept into the kingdom and spoke one terrible word — and every knight, from the mightiest champion to the newest banneret, turned to cold grey stone.

Every knight… but one.

Kenna Kuhn, the youngest squire in the castle, was beneath his notice. Too small. Too new. His mistake.

Now the curse pours out of the dark in endless waves of spirits, and only Kenna stands between Concur and the crown. Realm by realm she fights — the meadow, the courtyard, the great hall — gathering gold, waking frozen knights, and marching ever closer to the Throne Room.

Step 1: Concur your fears. Step 2: Free the knights. Step 3: Rule the kingdom.
