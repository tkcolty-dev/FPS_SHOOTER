How to Play

Goal

Destroy the enemy Castles. Lose yours and you're out.

Economy

Select a Builder and place Windmills next to wheat Farms — windmills near farms earn way more money.

Army

Barracks: swordsmen & archers · Stables: knights · Siege Workshop: catapults & hot air balloons 🎈 (flying — only archers, towers, and balloons can shoot them!) · Towers defend · 🧱 Walls connect into ramparts and block enemy ground troops (balloons float over).

Mouse & Keyboard

Left-drag: select · Click ground/enemy with units selected: move/attack (right-click works too) · B: build menu · WASD: camera · Wheel: zoom · Esc: cancel

Controller (Xbox)

Left stick: cursor · Right stick: camera · A: select/command (hold + move = box select) · B: cancel · X: build · Y: select army · RB: builders · LB: jump to castle · Triggers: zoom · D-pad: menu buttons

Modes

2v2: two devices, two players on each screen (2 controllers, or mouse + controller). 1v1: one device, split screen.
