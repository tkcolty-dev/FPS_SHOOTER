Scratch Together

The real Scratch editor, edited live by everyone in the room.

or join a room
Start a room, hit Copy link in the editor, send it to friends on the same wifi. Blocks, sprites, costumes and sounds sync instantly. File ▸ Load from your computer works too and brings everyone along.
